# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Blank is an engineering project with a public product surface. We expect
contributors, maintainers, and community members to communicate respectfully,
focus on facts, and keep discussions constructive.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful in language and tone.
- Assume good intent and ask clarifying questions before escalating.
- Keep technical disagreement focused on code, evidence, and user impact.
- Accept feedback and update proposals when better evidence is provided.
- Avoid personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, or intimidation.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, or abusive language.
- Doxxing or sharing private information without consent.
- Repeatedly derailing technical threads after moderator guidance.
- Sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention.
- Any conduct that would make a reasonable participant feel unsafe.

## Reporting

For conduct issues, open a private GitHub Security Advisory addressed to project
maintainers with the title prefix `Conduct:`. Do not open a public issue.

Please include:

- What happened
- Links or screenshots
- When it happened
- Any immediate safety concerns

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, lock threads, or block participation when this
policy is violated. Enforcement decisions prioritize participant safety and
project integrity.

## Scope

This policy applies to:

- GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and reviews
- Project docs, release notes, and linked community channels
